Spendlens privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Spendlens (app id app.spendlens.money) is a personal money tracker that keeps your data on your phone.

What the app reads

  • Text messages (SMS) from banks, cards and payment services, to find payments, balances, bills and autopays. Messages from personal numbers, one-time passwords and adverts are discarded without being saved.
  • Notifications from payment apps, only if you switch this on in Android's settings.
  • Photos of receipts and statement files, only when you choose one. Receipt text is read on the phone.

What stays on your phone

Your messages, the entries Spendlens works out from them, your budgets, goals and settings never leave your phone. Spendlens has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no crash reporting. We receive no information about you.

Ads

Spendlens shows one banner ad on the Accounts screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show it, the app uses the internet and Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device and ad interaction information to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. No message, amount, account, name or entry from Spendlens is given to the ad provider. In the EU, UK and similar regions Google asks for your consent first.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See Google's advertising policy.

What is stored

The entries Spendlens works out from your messages, your edits, budgets, goals and settings, saved in the app's own private storage on your phone. Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app.

Backups you make

"Back up" creates a file that you choose where to save. It contains your entries (including message text) and settings. It is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere private; anyone with the file can read it.

Your control

Delete everything from Settings > Your data, or uninstall the app. Revoke SMS access at any time in Android's app settings; the app then stops reading messages.

Children

Spendlens is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
